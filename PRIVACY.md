# Privacy Policy — Red Wedge Timer

Last updated: 11 September 2026

**Red Wedge Timer collects nothing.**

The app has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. It makes no network
requests of any kind — it has no networking code in it at all. Nothing you do in
the app leaves your device.

## What is stored on your device

One thing: when a timer is running, the app remembers the moment it will finish,
so that a timer survives the app being closed or the phone being locked. It is
stored in the app's own storage on your device, it is overwritten on the next
run, and it is removed when you delete the app. It is never transmitted anywhere.

## Sounds and notifications

The alarm sounds are generated by the app as it plays them; no recordings are
downloaded or uploaded. If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally by
iOS on your device so the alarm can reach you when the app is in the background.
No notification content is sent through any server.

## Children

The app is intended to be used by or with children, which is exactly why it
collects nothing: there is no data to protect, no profile to build, and no
third party receiving anything.
